The initiative

Research, responsibility and clinical reality belong in the same conversation.

The initiative has three aims: make antibiotic R&D visible through evidence, bring more stakeholder perspectives into the AMR response, and promote a precise, patient-specific understanding of stewardship.

01 — INNOVATE

Show the science, not just the claim

Make Wockhardt’s antibiotic research and development work visible through verified milestones, published evidence and approved facts, with clear context for clinical and global health audiences.

02 — ACT

Connect innovation with the wider AMR response

Bring doctors, patients, pharmacists, public health professionals, payers and researchers into a wider conversation about prevention, diagnosis, access, appropriate treatment and education.

03 — STEWARD

Make stewardship specific to the patient

Stewardship should support the right antibiotic for the right patient at the right time—not simply delay newer options by default. Decisions depend on severity, likely pathogens, microbiology where available, approved indications and reassessment.

02 / Our approach

Stewardship should guide the decision—not predetermine the delay.

Not newer at any cost. Not delay by default. Appropriate treatment, at the right time.

Treatment decisions remain the responsibility of qualified clinicians and must follow approved indications, local guidance and patient-specific assessment.

↗

Support antibiotic research and development

Scientific discovery and development are essential parts of the long-term response to resistant infections. New options must be supported by robust evidence and responsible use.

◎

Make stewardship specific to the patient

Stewardship means choosing and reviewing treatment for the individual patient. Decisions should consider severity, infection site, likely pathogens, local guidance, microbiology and susceptibility when available, approved indications and the need to reassess therapy as evidence evolves. It does not support routine use of any antibiotic class, nor does it make automatic delay a substitute for clinical judgement.

⌁

Bring perspectives together

Doctors, microbiologists, researchers, pharmacists, health systems, payers and public-health stakeholders all have a role in the AMR response.

✓

Put evidence at the centre

Campaign insights should be reported with clear methods and limitations. Clinical claims should be verifiable, balanced and reviewed before publication.

06 / A wider AMR response

AMR affects the whole healthcare system.

AMR cannot be addressed by prescribers or manufacturers alone. Progress depends on coordinated action across the people who discover, diagnose, prevent, treat, fund and experience infections.

01 / Science

Research and innovation

Advance discovery and development, generate robust evidence and communicate limitations transparently.

02 / Care

Clinical care and diagnostics

Support timely diagnosis, appropriate treatment choices, susceptibility-informed review and safe use.

03 / Systems

Patients and health systems

Strengthen awareness, prevention, equitable access, surveillance and system-level accountability.

The campaign invites perspectives from participating stakeholders. Responses will be reported with transparent methods and limitations and will not be presented as representative of all stakeholders or of India as a whole.

Wockhardt / Antibiotic R&D

A research programme built around unmet needs in infectious disease.

Wockhardt’s antibiotic research spans discovery, clinical development and regulatory milestones. The examples below are drawn from company-published information; each should be rechecked by Medical, Regulatory and Legal teams before external campaign use.

DISCOVERY & DEVELOPMENT
27+ years

Focused antibiotic innovation

Wockhardt described more than 27 years of antibiotic discovery and development efforts in its January 2026 announcement on WCK 5222.

Company announcement · 7 Jan 2026 ↗
CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT
ZAYNICH™

A novel antibiotic combination

Cefepime and zidebactam (WCK 5222) progressed through a global Phase III programme and received U.S. FDA approval in May 2026 for adults with complicated urinary tract infections, including pyelonephritis, caused by susceptible Gram-negative pathogens.

FDA approval announcement ↗
ANTIBIOTIC PORTFOLIO
Levonadifloxacin

Research translated into clinical use

Wockhardt’s official drug-discovery portfolio lists intravenous levonadifloxacin and oral alalevonadifloxacin as approved in India for adults with acute bacterial skin and skin structure infections, including diabetic foot infections and concurrent bacteraemia caused by susceptible isolates.

Company pipeline information ↗
RESPIRATORY INNOVATION
MIQNAF®

Nafithromycin for community-acquired pneumonia

Wockhardt discovered and developed nafithromycin, an oral antibiotic approved in India for community-acquired bacterial pneumonia (CABP) in adults. The approved regimen is once daily for three days. Its development adds a new macrolide-based treatment option in a field challenged by antimicrobial resistance.
